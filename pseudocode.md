PSEUDOCODE FOR DATA ANALYSIS

Step 1: Load the dataset
Load the dataset and explore the variables and observations within it.

Step 2: Clean the data
Check for missing values, duplicates, incorrect data types, and any other errors. Correct the data needed before starting the analysis.

Step 3: Calculate summary statistics
Calculate appropriate summary statistics. This could include the mean, median, standard deviation, minimum, and maximum, or quartiles in order to better understand the data.

Step 4: Create a visualization
Create an appropriate visualization to examine important patterns or relationships in the data.

Step 5: Interpret results
Review the statistics and visualizations and explain the main findings from the analysis.
